# Investment Intelligence

Personal market intelligence and investment learning system.

## Features

- Indian market data
- Global market data
- Gold and commodities
- Mutual fund tracking
- Macro-economic indicators
- Market-moving news
- Market analysis
- Valuation analysis
- Investment opportunity monitoring
- Daily reports
- Weekly reports
- Email notifications
